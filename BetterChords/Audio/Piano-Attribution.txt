BetterChords Piano is a piano-only extraction (bank 0, program 0) from
GeneralUser GS v2.0.3, by S. Christian Collins.
https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser
https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS

Modified for BetterChords by retaining only Grand Piano and its referenced samples,
including stereo links, loop data, generators, and modulators. Other instruments
and their sample data were removed; the retained piano samples are unchanged.
See GeneralUser-GS-LICENSE.txt for the original license.
Reproduce with scripts/extract_piano_soundfont.py and the upstream SoundFont.
